Brand guide · Version 2

The name, listening.

The MeetFly logo is the name itself, with a pair of headphones over the two e’s: MeetFly is the one listening in on your call. This page shows the logo, its rules, the colours and the type.

01 · Logo

One logo, four backgrounds

The headphones are always the accent colour: lime on dark, violet on light, because lime cannot be seen on a light background.

02 · Construction

How the headphones sit

  1. The band is a half-circle centred between the two e’s, then runs straight down each side.
  2. It rises above the letters to 0.92 of the type size, so the headphones read at a glance.
  3. An ear cup closes each end: a rounded bar over the outer edge of each e.
  4. Everything else is plain Outfit Bold, spaced slightly open so the headphones have room.

Clear space

The files already include the right margin. Keep at least the height of the letter e free around it.

Smallest sizes

Below 96px wide, use the icon instead of the full name.

Don’t recolour the headphones.

Don’t stretch it.

Don’t rotate it.

Don’t use the dark version on light.

05 · Type

Outfit, throughout

The logo is drawn from Outfit Bold, so headings use it too. Text is Outfit Regular. JetBrains Mono is for small labels and times. The sample words below are placeholders.

Lead · Outfit 400

A lead paragraph introduces a section in one or two sentences, a little larger than body text.

Body · Outfit 400

Body text is 16px with a 1.6 line height and lines of about 62 characters. Numbers such as 0123456789 and times such as 14:05 stay easy to read.
