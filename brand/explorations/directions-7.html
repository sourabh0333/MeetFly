Speaking and Voice M, rebuilt on a structure

These take the two you liked and tighten them. Each logo is still the name itself, leaning forward for speed, with voice bars built in. What is new: the bars now follow a rule in every option, the letters are bolder and more modern, and the app icon is drawn from the same parts as the name.

1 · Sliced M

One bold M, cut into five bars like a voice level. It still reads as an M at once, because the bars follow the letter’s real shape. The same M is the app icon, so the name and the icon are one drawing.

What changed from round 6

The Voice M was five loose bars and hard to read. It is now a real M cut into bars. Speaking’s bars were decoration at the end; they now line up with the letters’ heights.

The honest trade-off

Sliced M is the most ownable and gives the best icon. Speaking is the most readable. Block is the most unusual shape, but its name reads in two lines, which some people find slower.
