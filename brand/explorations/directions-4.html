Why these are better

The earlier rounds joined two icons. Each of these is one shape with one idea, drawn on a grid, tested at 16px, and clear in a single colour.

The honest trade-off

Little bird has the most personality and the best story, but birds are used by other brands, so the bubble shape must stay obvious. Paper bird is the most elegant and the least literal. Lift M is the safest.
