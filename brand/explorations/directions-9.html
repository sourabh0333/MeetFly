What each one shows

Every idea joins two of your three words: voice (microphone, sound wave, voice bars), speed or flight (wing, bird, plane, bolt, fast-forward) and the name (the M, or a meeting’s speech bubble).

About the colours

Four colour sets are spread across the ideas: violet, near-black with lime, blue, and orange-pink. Shape and colour are separate choices, so any idea can take any colour.

After you choose

I take your one or two numbers and draw them properly: cleaner curves, finer detail, the name beside it, small sizes and a one-colour version.
