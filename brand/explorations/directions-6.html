How your answers are used

Name only: the word is the logo, with no separate symbol. Speed: every letter leans forward. Voice: one detail in each option is a voice level or voice line.

The honest trade-off

Speaking keeps every letter of the name untouched, so it is the easiest to read. Voice M and Voice line are more distinctive, but the first letter takes a moment longer to read as an M.
