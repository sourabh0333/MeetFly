Yes, it is possible. “MeetFly” gives two clear pictures: Meet (a conversation, a speech bubble) and Fly (a wing, a plane). Each mark below joins the two. The wordmark does the same: “Meet” in the main colour, “Fly” in the accent. The three looks are the same as round 2. Choose a mark and they update.

How the name is used

Every mark is Meet and Fly joined into one shape, and the wordmark splits the same way, so the logo explains the name instead of decorating it.

The honest trade-off

Butterfly is the most original but needs a second look to see the bubbles. Winged bubble and Paper plane are instant but closer to familiar icons. MF is the simplest to recognise as yours.
