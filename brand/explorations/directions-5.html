How to read them

Bubble = a meeting. M = MeetFly. The bright detail = what MeetFly adds: the AI’s answer, the live voice, or your side of the talk.

The honest trade-off

M + spark is the easiest to understand; its small spark fades below 20px, so the favicon would use the bubble and M alone. Two voices m is the most original but takes a moment to read.
