Why these are different

Earlier rounds changed the shape of one idea. Here every card is a different idea and a different style of lettering, so one answer tells me a lot.

01, 04 and 12 say the most about the product. 07 is the most elegant but says the least. 02 and 11 are the quietest: the name does almost all the work.
