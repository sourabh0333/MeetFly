The two families

Inside the name: one logo, nothing extra, and more unusual. Beside the name: a badge that works alone as the app icon, and the name stays untouched.

The honest trade-off

A microphone is the clearest sign of “voice”, but many audio and podcast brands use one. Options 02 and 03 are the hardest to confuse with them, because the microphone is built into a letter.
