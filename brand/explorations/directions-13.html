The microphone in the name: eight more

Every option is the word itself, with one letter drawn as a microphone. This time each microphone is measured from the typeface, so it lines up with the letters around it. The pink dashed lines under each logo show the three lines everything sits on.

Three linesBaseline, small-letter height and capital height. Every part of a microphone starts or ends on one of them.
One widthThe capsule is as wide as the letters’ own strokes, so it has the same visual weight.
One accentOnly the capsule is coloured. Stand, handle and base stay the colour of the letters.

How to answer

Give me a number, or mix: “01, but in capitals”, “03 with the capsule from 07”.

The honest trade-off

The simpler the microphone (01, 08), the faster the name reads. The more complete the microphone (03, 07), the clearer the product, but the letter takes a moment longer to read.

After you choose

I refine the chosen letter, fix the spacing by eye, try colours, and build the logo files and the app icon.
