What changed from round 1

Every mark now contains a speech bubble, so it says “conversation” at a glance, plus one sign of the product: a spark for the AI reply, a sound wave for voice, a second bubble for the other side, or a live light.

The honest trade-off

Bubbles and sparks are common in AI and chat logos. They are clear, but less unique than an abstract mark. “Two voices” and “Live M” are the most distinctive of the four.
