What is in each card

The logo on dark, a small version on light, and the changed letter alone in a tile. That single letter becomes the app icon and favicon.

The honest trade-off

One changed letter keeps the name easiest to read. Two changed letters say more about the product, but the word takes slightly longer to read.

Still to decide

These are drafts in one typeface and one colour set. After you pick, I refine the letter shapes, try the colour, and build the final logo files.
