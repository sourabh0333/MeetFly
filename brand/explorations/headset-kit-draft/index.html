Brand guide · Version 2

The name, wearing a headset.

MeetFly’s logo is its own name, with a thin lime loop over the two e’s and a small microphone under them. This page shows the logo, its rules, the colours and the type. Every file it shows is in this folder.

02 · The icon

What the icon is

  1. The loop stands on the two e’s like the band of a pair of headphones. The e’s are the ear cups.
  2. The hook curves under the second e, like a headset’s microphone arm.
  3. The capsule at its end is the microphone.
  4. Together they say what MeetFly does: it sits in on your call and listens.

Clear space

Keep the height of the letter e free on every side. The logo files already include it.

Smallest sizes

Below 96px wide, use the symbol. Below 24px, use the favicon.

Don’t recolour the icon.

Don’t stretch it.

Don’t rotate it.

Don’t use the dark version on light.

05 · Type

Outfit, everywhere

Outfit is the logo’s own typeface, so the site and the app use it too: bold for headings, regular for reading. JetBrains Mono is for small labels and times. The sample words are placeholders.

A smaller heading for cards and lists
Lead · Outfit 400

A lead paragraph introduces a section in one or two sentences, a little larger than body text.

Body · Outfit 400

Body text is 16px with a 1.6 line height and lines of about 62 characters. Numbers such as 0123456789 and times such as 14:05 stay easy to read.
