How it lines up
MeetFly
  • The band comes down on the outer side of each e, the same distance from both, measured from the typeface.
  • The top of the band touches the capital line, level with the tops of the M and the F.
  • The band passes behind the letters, so it joins each e cleanly with no visible end.
  • One even thickness, about half the weight of the letters, so it never competes with the name.
