02 · Construction

How the symbol is built

  1. Two equal circles (dashed) round the top-left and bottom-right corners, so the shape leans forward.
  2. The tail is the square corner at the bottom left: the bubble points to the person speaking.
  3. The tip is the sharp corner at the top right, with the edge beneath it curving in like a wing.
  4. The live dot sits at the centre and is always Ember. In the one-colour versions it is cut out.

Clear space

Keep at least half the symbol's height free on every side.

Smallest sizes

Below 24px on a busy background, use the favicon: it carries its own tile.

Don't recolour the dot.

Don't stretch it.

Don't rotate it.

Don't add glows or shadows.

05 · Type

Sora and Manrope

Sora for headings, set tight. Manrope for everything you read. JetBrains Mono for small labels and times. The sample words below are placeholders.
