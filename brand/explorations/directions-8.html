MeetFly · Brand directions · Round 8

Three logo systems, built on a grid

I looked at where logo design is going this year and at how other voice and meeting products brand themselves, then started again. Each option is the letter M built from simple, equal parts that mean “voice”, beside the name in a clean modern typeface.

Built from partsThe current direction for tech logos is a mark made of simple, repeated shapes on a grid, so it stays sharp at any size.
Ready to moveLogos are now designed to animate. Each mark here has a natural motion; watch the large versions.
One odd detailPlain geometric logos all look alike. Each mark here has one thing only it does: a lit bar, a live dot, bars cut from its foot.

How the brief is met

Name: the M and the full word. Voice: the bars, dots and cut-outs are all sound levels. Speed: the marks move quickly and lightly; the letters stay upright so the logo stays structured.

The honest trade-off

Four bars is the most original but is read as “M” a beat slower than Solid. Signal is the most structured, but its thirteen dots blur below 20px, so a simpler favicon would be needed. Solid is the clearest and the least unusual.
