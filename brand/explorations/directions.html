How the marks were made

All three are new drawings, not based on the sample app icon. Each uses two colours only (a main shape and one accent dot or bar), so it works in one colour, on light or dark, and down to 16px.

What I left out

Serif headings and cream backgrounds (Meetily's look), green as an accent (their buy button), and generic microphone or camera icons.

Still to do once you pick

Final logo files (SVG, PNG, favicon, app icon), clear-space and size rules, the full colour scale with contrast checks, and a check that the mark isn't too close to an existing trademark.
